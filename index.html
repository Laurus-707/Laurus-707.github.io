Gekko - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026
Developer: Sergei Zybarev
Contact: zybarev@yahoo.com

Summary

Gekko helps you hand your unlocked phone to another person without your incoming messages, calls, and private notifications showing up. Gekko does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

What Gekko stores on your device

To do its job, Gekko keeps the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Hidden calls log. When a call is silently declined during a protected session, Gekko records its time and phone number so you can review it later. This list lives only on your device and can be cleared in the app at any time.
  • Session activity. A short on-device log of what happened during a session (for example, "a notification was hidden", or "an attempt to open Settings was blocked").
  • Your settings. The protection level, the list of apps whose notifications you chose to hide, the allowed-apps list for Level 3, theme, language, and home-screen-icon choice.
  • A snapshot of notification-channel settings of the apps you chose to hide, so the app can restore them exactly after a session.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling Gekko removes it.

What Gekko does NOT do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to any server.
  • It does not contain advertising or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • It does not read, store, or transmit the contents of your messages.
  • It does not monitor another person or track location.

Optional "Send report" feature

If you tap Send report, Gekko creates a diagnostic text file and lets you share it through an app you choose (for example, a messenger). This is always initiated by you. The report contains your device model, Android version, and Gekko's own technical log. It excludes message contents and contact names, and masks phone numbers. Gekko itself does not send anything automatically; you decide whether and where to share the file.

Permissions and why they are used

Gekko requests each permission only when you enable the feature that needs it:

  • Notification access - to hide incoming notifications during a session.
  • Companion device (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi association) - a technical requirement that lets Gekko silence notification channels without a visible icon flash. Gekko does not connect to, control, or exchange data with the chosen device.
  • Phone, Phone state, Contacts - to silently decline incoming calls during a session, including calls from saved contacts. Contacts are used only so the system routes such calls to Gekko's call filter; Gekko does not read, store, or upload your contacts.
  • Call log - to remove a call that Gekko declined during a session from the phone's call log and keep it in the in-app Hidden calls list (on your device only).
  • Usage access - to detect which app is currently open, so Level 3 can allow only the apps you permit.
  • Display over other apps - to show the block screen over a disallowed app on Level 3.
  • Biometric / device credential - to confirm it is you (the owner) before ending a session or changing protection.

Data retention and deletion

All Gekko data is stored locally. You can clear the Hidden calls list in the app, and uninstalling Gekko deletes all of its data from your device. Automatic backup of the app's data is disabled, so this data is not copied to the cloud.

Children

Gekko is a general-purpose privacy tool and is not directed at children.
